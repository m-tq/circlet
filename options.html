About
circlet
A Chromium extension for opening public and sealed Octra Circle applications, with Octra Name Service resolution and a deterministic oct address-bar keyword.
Version—
NetworksMainnet + Devnet
Circlespublic + sealed
Walletlocal webcli
Telemetrynone
Chrome extensions cannot register a native oct:// protocol handler or replace the address bar. Use the oct omnibox keyword, the popup, or this dashboard. circlet also intercepts oct:// links and .oct navigations.
